Come in, hero. Mind the bones... no, leave them. I arranged them terribly on purpose, once. That was back when I still rose before the black bells, when I taught the river to run uphill and signed my name across its moon. The scheme stands: at dusk my engines drink the sun, and your kingdom learns darkness by heart. Only... sit first. There. My voice keeps arriving late to my own threats. Hold that sword lower, it catches the candlelight and my eyes... Guards, why is nobody bringing cushions? Very well. Kneel, proud little world. But slowly. Everything slowly, today.
